# EPC schemes for new single-family dwellings: Germany (GEG/GModG) and Austria (OIB-Richtlinie 6)

Research date: 10 Nov 2026. Scope: new-build single-family houses (Wohngebäude, Gebäudekategorie 1 in AT).

Two big 2026 changes:
- **DE:** the GEG was renamed and amended as the **Gebäudemodernisierungsgesetz (GModG)**. It was promulgated in BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 226 on 28 Jul 2026. Article 1 has applied since 29 Jul 2026, and Article 2 (new primary energy factors, the reference building, DIN/TS 18599:2025-10 and Anlage 10a) applies from 1 Jan 2027.
- **AT:** **OIB-RL 6, edition September 2025**, replaces the A++..G / fGEE label with an A–G label that has a fossil/decarbonised split. The Länder are adopting it one by one in 2026.

## 1. Calculation method/standard and legal basis

### Takeaway
DE: GEG/GModG with DIN V 18599:2018-09. The DIN V 4108-6/4701-10 alternative for residential buildings expired on 31 Dec 2023, and DIN/TS 18599:2025-10 applies from 2027.
AT: OIB-RL 6 (2025) together with the OIB-Leitfaden "Energietechnisches Verhalten von Gebäuden". The calculation follows the ÖNORM B 8110-5 / B 8110-6-1 / H 5056-1 / H 5050-1 chain, a monthly method that the OIB has mapped explicitly to EN ISO 52000-1/52016-1. Each Land makes it binding through its own building-technical ordinance.

### Cited Findings
- **DE status:** the act is now titled "Gebäudemodernisierungsgesetz, GModG" on gesetze-im-internet.de. It was originally the GEG (BGBl. I 2020 S. 1728, in force since 1 Nov 2020), last amended by Art. 4 of the act of 23 Jul 2026 (BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 226). — [gesetze-im-internet § 15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/__15.html); [buzer Anlage 10 GModG](https://www.buzer.de/Anlage_10_GModG.htm)
- **DE timeline:** government draft BT-Drs. 21/6278 (8 Jun 2026); committee report BT-Drs. 21/7009; promulgated 28 Jul 2026. Art. 1 has applied since 29 Jul 2026, and Art. 2 and Art. 7 apply from 1 Jan 2027. These dates come from secondary sources. — [nexvyra GModG Anlage 10a](https://nexvyra.de/fakten/gmodg-anlage-10a-energieeffizienzklassen-nichtwohngebaeude); [Gleiss Lutz overview](https://www.gleisslutz.com/de/know-how/ueberblick-ueber-das-neue-gebaeudemodernisierungsgesetz)
- **DE § 20(1):** the primary energy demand of residential buildings is calculated with DIN V 18599:2018-09. Under § 20(2), DIN V 4108-6:2003-06 and DIN V 4701-10:2003-08 could be used until 31 Dec 2023 for uncooled buildings, using the reference climate of DIN V 18599-10 Annex E and 12.5 kWh/m²a of hot-water useful heat. That alternative has now expired. — [gesetze-im-internet § 20](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/__20.html)
- **DE from 1 Jan 2027:** DIN/TS 18599:2025-10 applies, together with the amended reference buildings. DIN SPEC 91606:2026-07 is cited for the reference floor area. This is from a secondary source and has not been verified against the BGBl. — [nexvyra](https://nexvyra.de/fakten/gmodg-anlage-10a-energieeffizienzklassen-nichtwohngebaeude)
- **AT legal basis:** OIB-RL 6, edition September 2025 (OIB-330.6-138/25), together with the OIB-Leitfaden "Energietechnisches Verhalten von Gebäuden" (edition September 2025). Tyrol published both as Anlage 6 of the TBV 2026 (Tir. LGBl. Nr. 59, 15 Jul 2026). — [RIS Tirol LGBl 59/2026 Anlage 6](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/LgblAuth/LGBLA_TI_20260715_59/Anlage_59_TBV2026_Anlage6_KM.pdf)
- **AT calculation chain (Leitfaden 2.1):**
  - climate model and use profiles: ÖNORM B 8110-5
  - heating and cooling demand (HWB, KB): ÖNORM B 8110-6-1
  - ventilation: H 5057-1
  - heating energy (HEB): ÖNORM H 5056-1
  - cooling: H 5058-1
  - lighting: H 5059-1
  - requirements, primary energy and CO2: ÖNORM H 5050-1

  — [RIS Tirol LGBl 59/2026 Anlage 6](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/LgblAuth/LGBLA_TI_20260715_59/Anlage_59_TBV2026_Anlage6_KM.pdf)
- **AT EN ISO mapping:** the OIB publishes a data sheet ("Datenblatt zur Eingabe und zur Verfahrensauswahl") that maps the Austrian method onto ÖNORM EN ISO 52000-1, 52003-1, 52010-1, 52016-1 and 52018-1 (2018 editions). The document is published "in Umsetzung" of those standards and states that it is not a legal codification of them. — same RIS document
- **AT time step:** the method is a monthly balance (monthly tables, e.g. "Monatlicher Lüftungswärmeübergangskoeffizient" and monthly PV coverage caps). An hourly climate can be derived only for heat-pump calculations. — same RIS document
- **AT Länder:**
  - Tyrol: TBV 2026, from July 2026. — [WKO Tirol](https://www.wko.at/tirol/information-consulting/ingenieurbueros/aenderungen-im-tiroler-baurecht-gebaeude-richtlinie)
  - Upper Austria: Oö. BauTV-Novelle 2026 makes RL 6 (2025) binding. — [Land OÖ](https://www.land-oberoesterreich.gv.at/Mediendateien/Formulare/Dokumente%20VerfD/O%c3%b6._BauTV-Novelle_2026_-_VO_Text_samt_Erl%c3%a4uterungen.pdf)
  - Vienna: W-EGUG 2026, with WBTV 2023 Anlage 13 referring to RL 6 (Sept 2025). — [WKO Wien](https://www.wko.at/wien/information-consulting/immobilien-vermoegenstreuhaender/wiener-eu-gebaeuderichtlinie-umsetzungsgesetz-2026)
- **AT, not all Länder in force yet:** the OVI noted that it was unclear whether and when every Land would adopt RL 6 (2025). Even the federal EAVG legislator doubted that all Länder would be done by 1 Jul 2026. — [OVI "Der Energieausweis neu"](https://www.ovi.at/fileadmin/Der_Energieausweis_neu.pdf)
- **AT, earlier Land differences:** implementation dates of RL 6 (2019) differed across the nine provinces. — [EPBD-CA Austria 2020](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373603634324878/Implementation-of-the-EPBD-in-Austria--2020.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Austrian method is closer to the target engine than the German one. It is a monthly quasi-steady-state balance with an official mapping to EN ISO 52016-1. DIN V 18599 is also a monthly method, but it has its own structure.
- During 2026–27, Austrian certificates may still be issued under RL 6 2019, 2023 or 2025 depending on the Land. The web app should state that it models the 2025 edition.

### Gaps
- I did not verify the adoption status of RL 6 (2025) in Lower Austria, Styria, Carinthia, Salzburg, Vorarlberg and Burgenland.
- I did not read the official BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 226 text. The 2027 GModG details come from secondary sources.

## 2. Label scale and exact thresholds

### Takeaway
DE residential: 9 classes, **A+ to H**, on final energy (kWh/m²a, per AN). The scale is **unchanged by the GModG**; only non-residential buildings get an A–G scale (Anlage 10a, from 2027).
AT (RL 6 2025): 7 classes, **A to G**, assessed on both HWB_Ref,SK and EEB_SK. There are separate "decarbonised" and "fossil" tracks, and **class A is reserved for decarbonised supply**. The old 9-class A++..G scale (2019/2023) used HWB, PEB, CO2 and fGEE.

  | A+ | ≤ 30 |

  | B | ≤ 75 |
  | C | ≤ 100 |
  | D | ≤ 130 |
  | E | ≤ 160 |
  | F | ≤ 200 |
  | G | ≤ 250 |
  | H | > 250 |

## 3. Indicator and reference floor area

### Takeaway
DE: **final energy (Endenergie) per m² Gebäudenutzfläche A_N**. A_N is a volume-derived area, not living area. Compliance is checked on non-renewable primary energy, and on total primary energy from 2027.
AT: **HWB_Ref,SK and EEB_SK per m² of conditioned gross floor area (BGF)**. PEB, CO2eq and the on-site renewable share are shown as numbers. fGEE is abolished.

### Cited Findings
- **DE:** A_N of a residential building is determined with DIN V 18599-1:2018-09 Eq. 30. Eq. 31 applies if the average storey height is > 3 m or < 2.5 m. — [gesetze-im-internet § 25](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/__25.html)
- **DE:** classes are based on final energy demand or consumption, in kWh/(m²·a) of Gebäudenutzfläche. — [buzer Anlage 10](https://www.buzer.de/Anlage_10_GModG.htm)
- **DE:** the compliance indicator is annual primary energy demand for heating, DHW, ventilation and cooling per A_N, and must be ≤ 0.55 × the reference building. — [gesetze-im-internet § 15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/__15.html)
- **AT:** the certificate's front page shows HWB_Ref and EEB on the efficiency scale, both for the site climate. Below them it gives EEB, PEB, operational GHG emissions, the share of on-site renewable energy, and (optional for now) life-cycle GWP. — [AEA/BMWET brochure](https://www.tirol.gv.at/fileadmin/themen/bauen-wohnen/baupolizei/20260219_Zentrale_%C3%84nderungen_der_OIB_Richtlinie_6_im_Jahr_2025.pdf)
- **AT:** fGEE is removed and replaced by EEB. Adverts must state HWB (site climate) and EEB (site climate) together with the class, e.g. "C fossil", from 1 Jul 2026 under the EAVG amendment. — [OVI](https://www.ovi.at/fileadmin/Der_Energieausweis_neu.pdf)
- **AT:** values are "Jahresbedarf pro Quadratmeter beheizter Brutto-Grundfläche". BGF and NGF are determined per ÖNORM B 1800 with details from B 8110-6-1. NGF may be approximated as 0.80 × BGF. — [RIS Tirol LGBl 59/2026 Anlage 6](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/LgblAuth/LGBLA_TI_20260715_59/Anlage_59_TBV2026_Anlage6_KM.pdf)
- **AT:** EEB for residential buildings includes heating, DHW, ventilation **and household electricity (HHSB)**, minus on-site yields. — same RIS document, energy certificate template text and point 4.4

### Inferences
- **DE A_N:** Eq. 30 is A_N = 0.32 m⁻¹ × V_e. This is my own knowledge of DIN V 18599-1 and is not confirmed by a fetched source. It typically gives 1.2–1.35× the living area, so the app needs a heated-volume input or a conversion.
- **AT:** EEB includes household electricity, so a heating/DHW-only engine must add a household-electricity default to reproduce EEB.
- **Area bases differ** (DE A_N from volume; AT gross floor area). The engine should calculate in absolute kWh and normalise per country.

### Gaps
- The exact household-electricity default in AT (ÖNORM H 5050 / B 8110-5) was not extracted.

## 4. Reference climate

### Takeaway
DE: a single reference climate, **Potsdam** (DIN V 18599-10 Annex E), is used for both compliance and the label.
AT: compliance uses the **Referenzklima (HGT = 3,400 Kd)**, while **label classes use the site climate (Standortklima, SK)**. Both come from ÖNORM B 8110-5, which has a climate model by location. Design indoor temperature and heating limit returned to 20 °C / 12 °C in 2025 (they were 22/14 in 2019/2023).

### Cited Findings
- **DE:** the reference climate is defined in DIN V 18599-10:2018-09 Annex E. — [gesetze-im-internet § 20](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/__20.html)
- **DE:** DIN V 18599-10 names Potsdam (region 4) as the German reference climate. This is a search-snippet summary of DIN/Beuth pages and was not read in full. — [DIN Media DIN V 18599-10](https://www.dinmedia.de/de/vornorm/din-v-18599-10/142651484)
- **AT:** requirements under point 4.4 are checked using the reference climate. — [RIS Tirol LGBl 59/2026 Anlage 6](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/LgblAuth/LGBLA_TI_20260715_59/Anlage_59_TBV2026_Anlage6_KM.pdf)
- **AT:** label class limits use HWB_Ref,SK and EEB_SK, "jeweils bezogen auf das Standortklima". Reference-climate and site-climate values come from ÖNORM B 8110-5 (Leitfaden 2.2). — same RIS document
- **AT:** the Austrian reference climate has HGT = 3,400 Kd and was set by averaging Austria's seven climate regions. — OIB cost-optimality document OIB-300-002/26, included in the same RIS PDF
- **AT:** design indoor temperature and heating limit are 12/20 before 2019, 14/22 in 2019 and 2023, and back to 12/20 in 2025. This lowers HWB by about 12 % depending on site. — [AEA/BMWET brochure](https://www.tirol.gv.at/fileadmin/themen/bauen-wohnen/baupolizei/20260219_Zentrale_%C3%84nderungen_der_OIB_Richtlinie_6_im_Jahr_2025.pdf); [OVI](https://www.ovi.at/fileadmin/Der_Energieausweis_neu.pdf) says about 10–13 %
- **AT, critique:** a University of Innsbruck presentation (2026) criticises the 2025 edition for outdated climate data among other things. — [UIBK Streicher](https://www.uibk.ac.at/media/filer_public/a1/38/a138cdc6-ab21-4056-a4b8-63a180f17ca0/prae_15-ibt-treff_260414_streicherwolfgang.pdf)

### Inferences
- **AT:** the app needs site-specific monthly climate data from ÖNORM B 8110-5 (paid) or an approximation. The label is not reference-climate based, unlike in Germany.

### Gaps
- Monthly temperature and irradiation values for the Potsdam and Austrian reference climates were not extracted. Both are in paid standards (DIN V 18599-10, ÖNORM B 8110-5).

## 6. Absolute thresholds or relative to a reference building

### Takeaway
DE: **label bands are absolute** (final energy). **Compliance is relative** to the GEG reference building (Q_P ≤ 0.55 × Q_P,Ref). From 2027 the reference building becomes technology-neutral.
AT: **label bands are absolute** (HWB, EEB). **Compliance uses absolute HWB limits that depend on geometry** (ℓc formulas), plus EEB_zul and PEB_zul derived from a reference equipment set.

### Cited Findings
- **DE § 15:** the new residential building's primary energy demand must be ≤ 0.55 × that of a reference building with the same geometry, A_N and orientation and the technology of Anlage 1. — [gesetze-im-internet § 15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/__15.html)
- **DE from 2027 (secondary):** the reference building becomes technology-neutral, with f_p,tot 0.75 until 2029 and 0.70 from 1 Jan 2030. It was previously a gas condensing boiler plus solar thermal. — [nexvyra Anlage 4](https://nexvyra.de/fakten/gmodg-22-anlage-4-primaerenergiefaktoren)
- **AT new residential, route 1** (reference climate, ℓc = V/A in m):
  - HWB_Ref,Bem,RK = 9 × (1 + 3.0/ℓc) from entry into force
  - 8 × (1 + 3.0/ℓc) from 1 Jan 2030 (zero-emission level; 1 Jan 2028 for public buildings)

  **Route 2:** HWB_Ref,max,RK = 13 × (1 + 3.0/ℓc), allowed if EEB_zul,RK and PEB_zul,RK are met through compensation measures. — [RIS Tirol LGBl 59/2026 Anlage 6](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/LgblAuth/LGBLA_TI_20260715_59/Anlage_59_TBV2026_Anlage6_KM.pdf)

  |---|---|---|---|
  | Small single-family house (ℓc = 1.0) | 36.0 kWh/m²a | 32.0 | 52.0 |

- **AT:** EEB_zul,RK and PEB_zul,RK are derived from HWB_Ref,Bem,RK, a carrier-dependent reference equipment set (point 8) and household electricity, with the solar-obligation yield deducted on both sides. — RIS document point 4.4

### Inferences
- Both labels can be computed without modelling a reference building, because the bands are absolute. A DE compliance check would need a reference-building run, which a shared engine can do with the same geometry and Anlage 1 technology.

## 7. Transposition of EPBD recast (EU) 2024/1275

### Takeaway
DE: transposition is late and partial. The GModG (July 2026) transposes the EPBD, but **keeps the residential A+..H final-energy scale**. The harmonised A–G scale with A = ZEB is introduced only for non-residential buildings (from 2027).
AT: OIB-RL 6 (2025) together with the EAVG amendment (in force 1 Jul 2026) introduce an A–G scale where **A requires decarbonised supply**, ZEB requirements from 2030 (2028 for public buildings), and a renovation pass. Adoption by the Länder is staggered.

## 9. Recommendation

### Takeaway
- **Germany: KEEP.** The label is a single absolute final-energy scale (A+..H, kWh/m²a A_N) with a single reference climate (Potsdam), and the thresholds are published in law. A simplified monthly engine can output final energy directly. Caveats: A_N ≈ 0.32·V_e (not living area); DIN V 18599 is paywalled; the 2027 factor and reference changes do not affect the residential class bands.
- **Austria: KEEP (with caveats; UNCERTAIN for exact replication).** The 2025 scale uses absolute HWB_Ref,SK and EEB_SK bands with a simple fossil/decarbonised flag, and the method is monthly and officially mapped to EN ISO 52016-1. The app should map to the worse of the HWB and EEB classes (the combination rule is unconfirmed). Caveats:
  - site climate (ÖNORM B 8110-5, paid) is needed rather than a single reference climate
  - EEB includes household electricity
  - 20 °C / 12 °C settings
  - Länder transition, so 2019/2023 certificates (A++..G, fGEE) coexist until about 2036

### Cited Findings
- See sections 1–8. DE bands: [buzer Anlage 10](https://www.buzer.de/Anlage_10_GModG.htm). AT bands: [RIS Tirol LGBl 59/2026 Anlage 6](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/LgblAuth/LGBLA_TI_20260715_59/Anlage_59_TBV2026_Anlage6_KM.pdf).

### Inferences
- A shared EN ISO 52016-style monthly engine can produce both: Germany's final energy per A_N, and Austria's HWB (heating need per BGF) plus EEB.
- The main country-specific layers are:
  - area normalisation
  - climate dataset
  - indoor setpoint (DE per DIN V 18599-10 profiles; AT 20 °C)
  - DHW defaults (DE 12.5 kWh/m²a A_N in the old method)
  - household electricity (AT only)
  - carrier flags (AT decarbonised/fossil)
- Expected deviation from official tools is moderate. It is acceptable for an educational web app but not for a legal certificate.

### Gaps
- No quantitative validation was found comparing a simplified EN ISO 52016 monthly engine against DIN V 18599 or ÖNORM B 8110-6-1 results.
