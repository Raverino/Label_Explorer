# EPC / energy label schemes for new single-family dwellings: Netherlands, Belgium (Flanders, Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg

Research date: 10 Nov 2026. Scope: new single-family dwellings. Where a value comes from a secondary source (consultancy, notary-hosted certificate, bank), it says so. Sources were checked through web search and fetch. Several official PDFs could only be read after local text extraction. The NL Bijlage IX table was read directly from wetten.overheid.nl (version in force from 06-10-2026).

## Q1. National calculation method and legal basis

### Takeaway
All five schemes use a national monthly-type method with its own legal basis. NL uses NTA 8800 under the Bbl and Omgevingsregeling. The three Belgian regions each run their own EPB/PEB method and software, sharing a common core. LU uses RGD 9 June 2021 with a reference-building method.

### Cited Findings
- **NL:** The label is computed with NTA 8800, a method based on the European CEN standards. All label obligations sit in the Besluit bouwwerken leefomgeving (Bbl, BWBR0041297). — [RVO Energielabel woningen](https://www.rvo.nl/onderwerpen/energielabel-woningen)
- **NL:** Art. 5.11 of the Omgevingsregeling governs the dwelling label. An energy adviser working for an NL-EPBD certificate holder under BRL 9500-W records the performance, using software attested under BRL 9501. The Minister then issues the label. — [Staatscourant 2026, 18123](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18123.pdf)
- **NL:** The Omgevingsregeling now refers to "NTA 8800 Energieprestatie van gebouwen 2025+C1:2026". Updated references to NTA 8800, BRL 9500 and BRL 9501 took effect 29 May 2026. — [Staatscourant 2026, 18123](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18123.pdf); [internetconsultatie EPG2026](https://internetconsultatie.nl/epg2026/document/14147)
- **NL:** Bbl art. 6.29 defines the label class. Lid 3 requires the label to show primary fossil energy use in kWh/(m²·yr). — [IPLO Energielabel gebouwen](https://iplo.nl/regelgeving/regels-voor-activiteiten/gebruiken-bouwwerk/rijksregels/energielabel-gebouwen/)
- **NL new-build requirements:** BENG 1/2/3 are set in Bbl arts. 4.148–4.149 (table 4.148A). — [Staatscourant 2026, 18123](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18123.pdf)
- **NL from 2030:** A "modernised determination method" will replace NTA 8800 from 1 Jan 2030, together with the new class scale. — [Staatscourant 2026, 18123 (toelichting)](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18123.pdf)
- **Flanders:** New builds get an "EPC Bouw" built from the EPB declaration. It shows the E-peil calculated under EPB regulations, and since building applications from 01.01.2020 also an energy label. The method and its factors are set in the Energiebesluit (arts. 9.1.8, 9.1.10, Bijlage V). — [vlaanderen.be EPC Bouw](https://www.vlaanderen.be/epc-bouw); [EPB-pedia EPC-bouw met energielabel](https://www.vlaanderen.be/epb-pedia/overzicht-van-epb-wijzigingen-per-jaar/epb-wijzigingen-vanaf-2020/epc-bouw-met-energielabel); [EPB-pedia karakteristiek primair energieverbruik](https://www.vlaanderen.be/epb-pedia/rekenmethode/rekenmethode-e-peil/karakteristiek-jaarlijks-primair-energieverbruik)
- **Flanders:** EPB declarations with EPC Bouw submitted from 9 Oct 2023 follow the current procedure. — [EPB-pedia EPC Bouw](https://www.vlaanderen.be/epb-pedia/taken-en-verantwoordelijkheden/epb-aangifte/epc-bouw)
- **Wallonia:** PEB regulation. New dwellings must meet the Q-ZEN level (Ew and Espec), described in the 2026 Q-ZEN guide. — [SPW brochure QZen 2026](https://energie.wallonie.be/files/PEB/B%c3%a2timents%20r%c3%a9sidentiels/Nouvelle%20construction/Outils/Conseils%20pratiques%20-%20publications/Guide%20pratique%20pour%20construire%20votre%20logement%20QZen/brochure%20QZen_2026.pdf)
- **Brussels:** "Réglementation travaux PEB". A PEB advisor enters the project in the PEB software. The current requirements apply to permit applications filed from 1 Apr 2024. — [Bruxelles Environnement, exigences travaux PEB](https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/reglementation/obligations-et-autorisations/exigences-et-procedure-de-la-reglementation-travaux-peb)
- **Brussels:** The certification protocol for residential certificates is published as "Protocole Certification PEB Résidentiel Livre I". — [Bruxelles Environnement](https://document.environnement.brussels/opac_css/elecfile/PROT_RES_Livre_I_v2.4_FR_vFINAL.PDF)
- **Luxembourg:** The Règlement grand-ducal of 9 June 2021 merged the 2007 residential and 2010 non-residential regulations. Annex I covers dwellings, Annex II functional buildings, Annex III general provisions. It has since been amended. — [renov.lu](https://renov.lu/en/energy-passport-luxembourg/energy-passport-sale-obligations/); [Chambre des Métiers / gov docs (search summary)](https://meco.gouvernement.lu/dam-assets/le-ministere/fonctions/energie/efficacite-energetique/FAQ-RGD-2021-Batiments-d-habitation.pdf)
- **Luxembourg:** The residential calculation covers heating, DHW, ventilation and auxiliary needs. Results are given as primary energy need, heating energy need and CO2 emissions. Since 2016, PV counts only for the share consumed by the building's technical equipment. Global requirements use a reference-building procedure. — [EPBD CA, Implementation of the EPBD in Luxembourg, Status 2020](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598207645919/Implementation-of-the-EPBD-in-Luxembourg--Status-in-2020.pdf)

### Inferences
- All schemes are monthly quasi-steady-state, in the EN ISO 13790/52016 family. NL NTA 8800 is explicitly a CEN EPB-standards implementation. That makes a shared simplified monthly engine plausible for computing energy need. The national system, auxiliary and primary-energy layers would have to be approximated.

### Gaps
- I could not open the LU RGD 2021 annex text on Legilux, so the exact LU method (DIN V 18599-like or not) and software name are unconfirmed.
- I did not retrieve the Wallonia or Brussels method annex documents themselves.

## Q2. Label scale and class thresholds

### Takeaway
NL has the only official, primary-source threshold table found (A++++ to G on EP-2 kWh/m²·yr). LU (A+ to I), Wallonia (A++ to G) and Brussels (A++ to G with sub-levels) have absolute kWh/m² scales, but they were confirmed only through secondary sources. Flanders uses A+ to F and confirms officially only that A is about 100 kWh/m².

### Cited Findings

**Netherlands, Omgevingsregeling Bijlage IX, Tabel 1 (dwellings), primary fossil energy use EP2 in kWh/m²·yr (version in force from 06-10-2026)** — [wetten.overheid.nl Omgevingsregeling Bijlage IX](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0045528&bijlage=IX)

| Class | EP2 (kWh/m²·yr) |
|---|---|
| A++++ | ≤ 0,00 |
| A+++ | 0,01–50,00 |
| A++ | 50,01–75,00 |
| A+ | 75,01–105,00 |
| A | 105,01–160,00 |
| B | 160,01–190,00 |
| C | 190,01–250,00 |
| D | 250,01–290,00 |
| E | 290,01–335,00 |
| F | 335,01–380,00 |
| G | > 380,00 |

- **NL "A0" (ZEB) mark, Bijlage IXa.** The mark exists for new buildings whose permit application was filed after 29 May 2026. It requires EP2 ≤ 27 kWh/m²·yr for "andere woonfunctie" (single-family houses) and ≤ 45 for a woongebouw. The building must also meet BENG 1 and BENG 3 from Bbl table 4.148A and cause no on-site fossil CO2 emissions. EP2 is set 10% below BENG 2 (EPBD art. 11(3)). — [Staatscourant 2026, 18123](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18123.pdf); [wetten.overheid.nl Bijlage IXa](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0045528&bijlage=IX)
- **NL BENG for ground-bound dwellings, RVO 2019 table:**
  - **BENG 1 (energy need, kWh/m²·yr):**
    - Als/Ag ≤ 1.5: ≤ 55
    - 1.5 < Als/Ag ≤ 3.0: ≤ 55 + 30·(Als/Ag − 1.5)
    - Als/Ag > 3.0: ≤ 100 + 50·(Als/Ag − 3.0)
  - **BENG 2 (primary fossil energy):** ≤ 30 kWh/m²·yr.
  - **BENG 3 (renewable share):** ≥ 50%.
  - Als is the heat-loss envelope area and Ag the usable floor area.
  - Sources: [RVO De BENG eisen juni 2019](https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2019/06/De%20BENG%20eisen%20juni%202019.pdf); [Rockwool whitepaper BENG](https://www.rockwool.com/syssiteassets/rw-bnl/downloads-nl/downloads/whitepapers/beng/whitepaper-ontwerpen-en-bouwen-volgens-beng-nl.pdf); [omgevingsweb](https://www.omgevingsweb.nl/wp-content/uploads/po-assets/282597.pdf)
- **NL lightweight construction:** Ground-bound dwellings with a lightweight construction get a +5 kWh/m²·yr allowance on BENG 1 (secondary). — [Rockwool whitepaper](https://www.rockwool.com/syssiteassets/rw-bnl/downloads-nl/downloads/whitepapers/beng/whitepaper-ontwerpen-en-bouwen-volgens-beng-nl.pdf)
- **NL consistency check:** The A0 limit of 27 = 0.9 × 30 matches BENG 2 = 30 for "andere woonfunctie". That supports BENG 2 = 30 still being in force in 2026. — [Staatscourant 2026, 18123](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18123.pdf)

**Flanders**
- **Label A anchor (official):** "E60 komt in de meeste gevallen immers overeen met een primair verbruik van 100 kWh/m² per jaar". Scoring E60 or lower means label A. — [EPB-pedia EPC-bouw met energielabel](https://www.vlaanderen.be/epb-pedia/overzicht-van-epb-wijzigingen-per-jaar/epb-wijzigingen-vanaf-2020/epc-bouw-met-energielabel)
- **Residential scale (secondary, a broker site citing VEKA), kWh/m²·yr primary energy:** A+ < 0; A 0–100; B 100–200; C 200–300; D 300–400; E 400–500; F > 500. — [search summary; e.g. bobex.be](https://www.bobex.be/nl-be/epc-certificaat/waarde/)
- Flanders uses an A–F scale introduced 1 Jan 2019. — [Test-Aankoop, 16 Apr 2026](https://www.test-aankoop.be/woning-energie/isolatie/nieuws/epc-label-a-emissievrije-gebouwen-2030)
- **New-build requirements:** E30 max; S28 max; minimum renewable energy 15 kWh/m²·yr (secondary, citing vlaanderen.be, dated 17 Jun 2026). A Flemish publication on 2024 requirements cites 20 kWh/m²·yr, so the renewable minimum is conflicting. — [callmepower.be EPB](https://callmepower.be/nl/verhuizen/energie/epb); [EPB-eisentabel vanaf 2025](https://assets.vlaanderen.be/image/upload/v1718208486/epbeisentabel_vanaf2025_rnzx2j.pdf)

- **New-build Q-ZEN** (since 1 Jan 2021, replacing Ew 65 / Espec 115): Ew ≤ 45 and Espec ≤ 85 kWh/m²·yr. A compliant new home is therefore at best class A by Espec. — [SPW brochure QZen 2026](https://energie.wallonie.be/files/PEB/B%c3%a2timents%20r%c3%a9sidentiels/Nouvelle%20construction/Outils/Conseils%20pratiques%20-%20publications/Guide%20pratique%20pour%20construire%20votre%20logement%20QZen/brochure%20QZen_2026.pdf)

**Brussels (secondary: certificate posted on a notary site; IEA policy entry)**
- Residential bands in kWh EP/m²·yr: A ≤ 45; B 46–95; C 96–150; D 151–210; E 211–275; F 276–345; G > 345. Classes A to E each have +/−/0 sub-levels. — [notary-hosted Brussels PEB certificate](https://immo.notaire.be/immoplatform-public-service_v1/api/attachments/356739/289057/730423.pdf)
- The IEA 2011 entry gives A++ < 0, A+ 0–15, A 16–30, A− 31–45, B+ 46–62, B 63–78, …, G > 345. This is consistent with the sub-levels splitting the band A ≤ 45 into thirds. The IEA table has some gaps and overlaps. — [IEA policy 2493](https://www.iea.org/policies/2493)
- **New-build requirements:** CEP (total primary energy) ≤ 45 kWh/m²·yr; net heating need ≤ 15 kWh/m²·yr; limited overheating (6500 Kh/yr cited by Test-Achats). These apply to applications from 1 Apr 2024. Gas and oil boilers are banned for applications from 1 Jan 2025. — [Bruxelles Environnement exigences](https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/reglementation/obligations-et-autorisations/exigences-et-procedure-de-la-reglementation-travaux-peb); [Test-Achats PEB](https://www.test-achats.be/maison-energie/isolation/dossier/peb)
- Existing-stock targets: PEB 275 kWh/m²·yr by 1 Jan 2033 and PEB 150 by 31 Dec 2045. — [Bruxelles Environnement objectifs PEB](https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/reglementation-et-inspection/obligations-et-autorisations/objectifs-peb-pour-chaque-logement-et-exigences-peb-en-cas-de-travaux)

**Luxembourg (secondary: renov.lu, citing Annex I RGD 2021)**

Single-family house, primary energy in kWh/m²·yr. renov.lu warns that tables showing "A ≤ 45" are outdated. — [renov.lu Energy passport](https://renov.lu/en/energy-performance-certificate/)

| Class | Primary energy (kWh/m²·yr) |
|---|---|
| A+ | ≤ 22 |
| A | ≤ 41 |

- The LU certificate shows three classes, A+ to I: energy efficiency (primary energy), thermal insulation (heating need) and environmental (CO2). Apartment buildings have their own scales. — [renov.lu](https://renov.lu/en/energy-performance-certificate/); [EPBD CA LU 2020](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598207645919/Implementation-of-the-EPBD-in-Luxembourg--Status-in-2020.pdf)
- The residential EPC classes were deliberately kept unchanged when requirements tightened (status 2020) "to ensure the comparability". — [EPBD CA LU 2020](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598207645919/Implementation-of-the-EPBD-in-Luxembourg--Status-in-2020.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Two- vs three-indicator labels:** NL, Flanders, Wallonia and Brussels map one primary-energy indicator per m² onto letters. LU shows three letters; a single-indicator app would map only the primary-energy class.
- **Possible LU size dependence:** The LU thresholds may depend on building size or compactness. renov.lu gives one table for single-family houses, and apartments have separate scales, so treat the single table as approximate until the Annex I text is checked.
- **NL EP2 vs the others:** NL EP2 counts only fossil primary energy, so PV and heat pumps push houses to A+++/A++++. The Belgian and LU scales use total or non-renewable primary energy with different factors. The same building will land in very different letters, which is fine for an app that maps per country.

### Gaps
- No official (government) threshold tables were retrieved for Flanders, Wallonia, Brussels or LU. Values come from accredited certifiers, real certificates, IEA or renov.lu.
- I did not confirm whether LU class thresholds depend on A/V or building size.
- The current Bbl table 4.148A was not read directly. The BENG values come from RVO 2019 and are cross-checked indirectly by the 2026 A0 limit.

## Q3. Indicator and reference floor area

### Takeaway
NL uses fossil primary energy per m² of usable floor area (Ag). Belgium's regions use characteristic primary energy per m², with E-peil/Ew as a ratio to a reference value for requirements. LU uses primary energy per m² of energy reference area An, with reference-building requirements.

### Cited Findings
- **NL:** EP2 is "primair fossiel energiegebruik in kWh per m2 gebruiksoppervlak per jaar". — [RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/onderwerpen/energielabel-woningen)
- **NL:** EP2 sums primary energy for heating, cooling, DHW and ventilation, minus on-site renewable generation. — [RVO BENG indicatoren](https://www.rvo.nl/onderwerpen/wetten-en-regels-gebouwen/beng/indicatoren)
- **NL:** From 2030 the label letter will be based on total primary energy use instead of primary fossil energy use. — [IPLO toelichting EPBD IV](https://iplo.nl/thema/energiebesparing/ontwikkeling-wetgeving-energiebesparing/ontwikkelingen-europese-richtlijn-epbd-iv/toelichting-per-artikel-epbd-iv/)
- **Flanders:** The label uses the karakteristiek jaarlijks primair energieverbruik per m². The E-peil is computed alongside it. — [EPB-pedia](https://www.vlaanderen.be/epb-pedia/rekenmethode/rekenmethode-e-peil/karakteristiek-jaarlijks-primair-energieverbruik)
- **Wallonia:** Espec is specific primary energy consumption per m² of heated floor area. Ew is the ratio of the building's total annual primary consumption to that of a reference building. — [SPW brochure QZen 2026](https://energie.wallonie.be/files/PEB/B%c3%a2timents%20r%c3%a9sidentiels/Nouvelle%20construction/Outils/Conseils%20pratiques%20-%20publications/Guide%20pratique%20pour%20construire%20votre%20logement%20QZen/brochure%20QZen_2026.pdf); [Certinergie](https://www.certinergie.be/?p=19005)
- **Brussels:** Expressed in kWh of primary energy per m² of dwelling. — [notary-hosted certificate](https://immo.notaire.be/immoplatform-public-service_v1/api/attachments/356739/289057/730423.pdf)
- **Luxembourg:** Requirements are checked against a reference building with the same geometry and location but reference U-values and airtightness. The FAQ refers to the energy reference area An. — [FAQ RGD 2021 bâtiments d'habitation](https://meco.gouvernement.lu/dam-assets/le-ministere/fonctions/energie/efficacite-energetique/FAQ-RGD-2021-Batiments-d-habitation.pdf); [EPBD CA LU 2020](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598207645919/Implementation-of-the-EPBD-in-Luxembourg--Status-in-2020.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Reference areas differ:** NL Ag, BE heated floor area (Ach), LU An. The app should let the user input one floor area and apply simple conversion factors, or accept the bias.

### Gaps
- The exact Belgian floor-area definitions (Ach / bruikbare vloeroppervlakte) and the LU An definition were not extracted verbatim.

## Q5. Primary energy factors

### Takeaway
Only Flanders' factors were confirmed from an official page: electricity 2.5, fossil fuels 1. NL, Wallonia, Brussels and LU factors remain unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **Flanders:** "Voor elektriciteit is die factor 2,5" (60% production/transport losses). Fossil fuels (gasoil, gas) get factor 1. District heating is not stated. — [EPB-pedia karakteristiek primair energieverbruik](https://www.vlaanderen.be/epb-pedia/rekenmethode/rekenmethode-e-peil/karakteristiek-jaarlijks-primair-energieverbruik)
- **NL:** The NTA 8800:2026 consultation says externally supplied energy always has a total primary energy factor ≥ 1. For the ZEB calculation, grid electricity gets a different factor than locally generated electricity. — [internetconsultatie EPG2026](https://internetconsultatie.nl/epg2026/document/14147)
- **LU:** The move from RGD 2007 to RGD 2021 changed some primary energy factors and environmental factors, which can shift classes. For district heating, the factor is a weighted average of the sources by coverage share when waste heat is used. Otherwise a tabulated value applies. — [FAQ RGD 2021 bâtiments d'habitation](https://meco.gouvernement.lu/dam-assets/le-ministere/fonctions/energie/efficacite-energetique/FAQ-RGD-2021-Batiments-d-habitation.pdf)

### Gaps
- **NL fossil factors:** The commonly cited values (electricity 1.45, gas 1.0) were not confirmed from an official source in this run. They are in NTA 8800 tables 5.2 / AB.2, which are behind NEN.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** Electricity factors were not confirmed. Wallonia reportedly moved away from 2.5, but this is unverified.
- **LU:** Numeric factors were not extracted.
- **District heating:** Not confirmed for any of the five schemes.

## Q6. Absolute thresholds or relative to a reference building?

### Takeaway
Labels are absolute kWh/m²·yr scales in all five schemes. New-build requirements are relative in BE (E-peil / Ew) and LU (reference building). NL requirements are absolute (BENG 2 = 30), apart from the shape-dependent BENG 1.

### Cited Findings
- **NL:** Absolute EP2 bands. — [Bijlage IX](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0045528&bijlage=IX)
- **NL:** BENG 1 depends on Als/Ag. — [RVO 2019](https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2019/06/De%20BENG%20eisen%20juni%202019.pdf)
- **Flanders:** The label is absolute kWh/m². The E30 requirement is a relative E-peil. — [EPB-pedia](https://www.vlaanderen.be/epb-pedia/overzicht-van-epb-wijzigingen-per-jaar/epb-wijzigingen-vanaf-2020/epc-bouw-met-energielabel)
- **Wallonia:** Ew is a ratio to a reference building. Espec and the label are absolute. — [SPW QZen 2026](https://energie.wallonie.be/files/PEB/B%c3%a2timents%20r%c3%a9sidentiels/Nouvelle%20construction/Outils/Conseils%20pratiques%20-%20publications/Guide%20pratique%20pour%20construire%20votre%20logement%20QZen/brochure%20QZen_2026.pdf)
- **Brussels:** Absolute CEP ≤ 45 and BNC ≤ 15. — [Bruxelles Environnement](https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/reglementation/obligations-et-autorisations/exigences-et-procedure-de-la-reglementation-travaux-peb)
- **LU:** NZEB compliance comes from the reference-building method. The label scale is absolute per renov.lu. — [renov.lu](https://renov.lu/en/energy-performance-certificate/); [EPBD CA LU 2020](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598207645919/Implementation-of-the-EPBD-in-Luxembourg--Status-in-2020.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Mapping difficulty:** The label mapping is simple in all five (absolute bands). The Flemish E-peil and Walloon Ew requirement checks would need the reference-value formulas, which were not collected here.

## Q7. Transposition of the EPBD recast (EU) 2024/1275

### Takeaway
NL used the art. 19(2) derogation. It keeps A++++ to G until 2030 and introduced an "A0" ZEB mark for new buildings from 29 May 2026. Flanders also qualifies for the derogation (scale changed 2019) and has not announced timing. Wallonia and Brussels already use A–G, but whether A will be reserved for zero-emission buildings is unclear. For LU no transposition details were found.

### Cited Findings
- **EU rules:** EPCs must use an A–G scale with A = zero-emission, and comply by 29 May 2026. Member states that rescaled between 1 Jan 2019 and 28 May 2024 may postpone to 31 Dec 2029. — [Test-Aankoop](https://www.test-aankoop.be/woning-energie/isolatie/nieuws/epc-label-a-emissievrije-gebouwen-2030)
- **NL derogation:** "De huidige indeling in energielabelklassen ... blijft dus behouden tot 2030". NL relied on art. 19(2) because it rescaled on 1 Jan 2021. — [Staatscourant 2026, 18123](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18123.pdf)
- **NL new scale from 2030:** The new class scale applies from 1 Jan 2030 with the modernised method. The plus classes disappear and the scale becomes A–G. The indicator switches to total primary energy. — [Staatscourant 2026, 18123](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18123.pdf); [IPLO](https://iplo.nl/thema/energiebesparing/ontwikkeling-wetgeving-energiebesparing/ontwikkelingen-europese-richtlijn-epbd-iv/toelichting-per-artikel-epbd-iv/)
- **NL zero-emission new builds:** New public buildings from 1 Jan 2028; all new buildings from 1 Jan 2030. — [IPLO](https://iplo.nl/thema/energiebesparing/ontwikkeling-wetgeving-energiebesparing/ontwikkelingen-europese-richtlijn-epbd-iv/toelichting-per-artikel-epbd-iv/)
- **NL label redesign:** The new label design applies from 29 May 2026. The label is also required at major renovation and at rental renewal. — [RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/onderwerpen/energielabel-woningen)
- **NL text note:** The Staatscourant text writes "A+++++ tot en met G" in the toelichting. The binding Bijlage IX table for dwellings tops out at A++++, while the utility-building table X has A+++++. — [Staatscourant 2026, 18123](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-18123.pdf); [Bijlage IX/X](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0045528&bijlage=IX)
- **Flanders:** A redistribution to A–G is expected, but no timing has been announced (as of 16 Apr 2026). Gas is banned in new builds in Flanders and Brussels from 1 Jan 2025. Wallonia has no gas ban yet; its oil ban starts 1 Jan 2026. — [Test-Aankoop](https://www.test-aankoop.be/woning-energie/isolatie/nieuws/epc-label-a-emissievrije-gebouwen-2030)
- **Brussels:** Zero-emission level for every new dwelling from 1 Jan 2030 (by permit filing date), and for new public buildings from 2027. — [Bruxelles Environnement](https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/reglementation/obligations-et-autorisations/exigences-et-procedure-de-la-reglementation-travaux-peb)
- **Brussels and Wallonia:** They already use A–G. Test-Aankoop asks them to clarify whether their scales will change. — [Test-Aankoop](https://www.test-aankoop.be/woning-energie/isolatie/nieuws/epc-label-a-emissievrije-gebouwen-2030)

### Inferences
- **Two regimes for any NL model:** Pre-2030 (EP2, A++++ to G) and post-2030 (total primary energy, A–G, thresholds not yet published). The Belgian regions may rescale before 2030 as well.

### Gaps
- No information was found on LU transposition (A–G rescaling from A+ to I, ZEB class).
- Wallonia and Brussels plans for the A = ZEB reclassification were not found.

## Q8. Documentation quality

### Takeaway
NL is the best documented for thresholds: an official, free legal table plus a published A0 rule. The method (NTA 8800) is a NEN document, and free access was not verified. The Belgian regions and LU publish requirements on official sites, but the label tables were hard to find on official pages. The EPBD CA country reports are English but dated (LU 2020).

## Q9. Recommendation

### Takeaway
- **NL: KEEP.**
- **Wallonia: KEEP (with caveats).**
- **Brussels: KEEP (with caveats).**
- **Flanders: UNCERTAIN.**
- **Luxembourg: UNCERTAIN.**

### Cited Findings
- **NL: KEEP.** The official EP2 label table, A0 limit and BENG limits including the Als/Ag formula are public. The EP2 fossil-factor logic needs the NTA 8800 factors (gap). It is also the user's home-country reference. — [Bijlage IX](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBR0045528&bijlage=IX); [RVO BENG 2019](https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2019/06/De%20BENG%20eisen%20juni%202019.pdf)
- **Wallonia: KEEP (with caveats).** The complete absolute Espec scale A++ to G is consistent across certificates. The new-build Q-ZEN limits (Ew 45 / Espec 85) are official. Primary energy factors are still to be confirmed. — [Certinergie](https://www.certinergie.be/en/energy-performance-certificate/epc-certificate-wallonia/); [SPW QZen 2026](https://energie.wallonie.be/files/PEB/B%c3%a2timents%20r%c3%a9sidentiels/Nouvelle%20construction/Outils/Conseils%20pratiques%20-%20publications/Guide%20pratique%20pour%20construire%20votre%20logement%20QZen/brochure%20QZen_2026.pdf)
- **Brussels: KEEP (with caveats).** It has an absolute kWh EP/m² scale and official new-build CEP ≤ 45 / BNC ≤ 15. The sub-level thresholds come from certificates and IEA, not an official table. — [Bruxelles Environnement](https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/reglementation/obligations-et-autorisations/exigences-et-procedure-de-la-reglementation-travaux-peb); [IEA](https://www.iea.org/policies/2493)
- **Flanders: UNCERTAIN.** Electricity factor 2.5 and the A ≈ 100 kWh/m² anchor are official, but the full scale is only from secondary sources. A rescale to A–G is pending with unknown timing. Every compliant new build (E30, no gas) will sit in A or A+, so the label differentiates little for new builds. — [EPB-pedia](https://www.vlaanderen.be/epb-pedia/overzicht-van-epb-wijzigingen-per-jaar/epb-wijzigingen-vanaf-2020/epc-bouw-met-energielabel); [Test-Aankoop](https://www.test-aankoop.be/woning-energie/isolatie/nieuws/epc-label-a-emissievrije-gebouwen-2030)
- **Luxembourg: UNCERTAIN.** The thresholds come only from renov.lu. Possible size/compactness dependence and three parallel classes are unverified. Primary energy factors and EPBD-recast plans were not found. Reading Legilux RGD 2021 Annex I would be needed to upgrade it to KEEP. — [renov.lu](https://renov.lu/en/energy-performance-certificate/); [EPBD CA LU 2020](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598207645919/Implementation-of-the-EPBD-in-Luxembourg--Status-in-2020.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Fit with a shared engine:** All five map an absolute kWh/m²·yr primary-energy value to letters, so a shared monthly engine plus per-country primary energy factors and band tables is feasible.
- **Main error sources:** Per-country primary energy factors (NL fossil-only vs BE total), system efficiencies, floor-area definitions and the reference climate. These matter more than the label tables.

### Gaps
- Official threshold tables for BE and LU, and primary energy factors for NL, Wallonia, Brussels and LU, should be confirmed before implementation.
