# EPC schemes for new single-family dwellings: Denmark, Sweden, Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania

Research date: 2026-11-10. Scope: can each country's EPC label be reproduced by one shared simplified monthly EN ISO 52016/52000-style engine, with the result mapped onto the national scale? Primary legal texts were read where they could be downloaded (BFS 2026:2, FI decree 1048/2017, EE regulation 36 annex 3 (2026 wording), the DK BR18 §259 text and guidance, ENS methodology note). Latvia and Lithuania class tables could not be retrieved from primary sources (see Gaps).

## 1. National calculation method / standard and legal basis

### Takeaway
DK and FI use national quasi-steady-state methods (DK: SBi 213/BUILD 213 with the Be18 tool, monthly; FI: Ministry of Environment decree plus a calculation guideline). SE uses BEN (calculated or measured, normalised). In SE a completely new method (Etal) started on 1 Oct 2026. EE uses regulation 58 (simulation-based). LV uses Cabinet Reg. 222/2021. LT uses STR 2.01.02:2016.

### Cited Findings
- **DK:** the energy frame calculation must follow SBi-anvisning 213 "Bygningers energibehov". This is the method behind the Be18 tool, and the guidance says Be18 may be used. Degree-hours and solar gains come from the Danish Design Reference Year (DRY) — [bygningsreglementet.dk guidance, Energiforbrug §250–298](https://www.bygningsreglementet.dk/media/mxzlfopn/energiforbrug.pdf)
- **DK:** BR18 ch. 11 is structured as: §250 "Energiforbrug og klimapåvirkning", §§251–256 "Bygningers energiforbrug", §259 energy frames for dwellings, §§261–266 requirements when the frame is used. Low-energy class is ch. 25 (§§473–484). The site lists separate consolidated BR18 versions for 1/1–30/6 2025 and 1/7–31/12 2025 — [bygningsreglementet.dk ch. 11 index](https://www.bygningsreglementet.dk/Tekniske-bestemmelser/11/Krav)
- **SE (old rules):** BBR (BFS 2011:6) section 9 sets the requirement as a primärenergital (EP_pet). Energy use is determined with BFS 2016:12 BEN, either by calculation or by measurement, normalised to normal use and a normal year — [Boverket, Primärenergital och byggnadens energiprestanda](https://www.boverket.se/sv/byggande/bygg-och-renovera-energieffektivt/energihushallningskrav/primarenergital-och-byggnadens-energiprestanda/)
- **SE (new rules):** new energy rules apply from 1 Oct 2026 (BFS 2026:9, "energihushållning och värmeisolering i byggnader"). They are part of Sweden's transposition of the recast EPBD and bring a new calculation method for energy performance, a new electric-power requirement and a solar-technology requirement. Until 30 Sep 2027 the builder may choose the older rules, with some exceptions. The rules will be amended five times up to 2033 — [Boverket news, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.boverket.se/sv/om-boverket/nyheter-aktuellt/nyheter/energihushallning-och-energideklaration/)
- **SE:** for energy declarations, primärenergital is replaced by energiprestandatal (Etal), which is meant to match the actual energy bill more closely — [Boverket news, decision](https://www.boverket.se/sv/om-boverket/nyheter-aktuellt/nyheter/nya-krav-pa-energihushallning-och-energideklarationer-beslutade/) (search snippet; the full page body could not be extracted)
- **FI:** Ympäristöministeriön asetus rakennuksen energiatodistuksesta 1048/2017, issued under the Energy Certificate Act 50/2013. E-luku inputs come from the Ministry's energy calculation guideline (energiatehokkuuden laskentaohje) — [Finlex 1048/2017 PDF](https://finlex.fi/api/media/statute/53865/mainPdf/main.pdf)
- **FI:** a March 2026 consultation draft would amend 1048/2017 (title, several sections, annexes 1–4) for the recast EPBD — [hankeikkuna consultation](https://api.hankeikkuna.fi/asiakirjat/b103d72a-1885-419e-81ab-59413fa42d78/425104b6-977a-44f8-8e5a-66595966a996/LAUSUNTOPYYNTO_20260318122144.PDF) (search snippet only)
- **EE:** ETA is calculated under regulation 58 "Hoone energiatõhususe arvutamise metoodika" (5 June 2015). Labels follow regulation 36 "Nõuded energiamärgise andmisele ja energiamärgisele" (30 Apr 2015). The class table is annex 3, worded by infrastructure-minister regulation no. 63 of 09.07.2026. Minimum requirements are in regulation 63 (11 Dec 2018) "Hoone energiatõhususe miinimumnõuded" — [RT annex 3](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/aktilisa/1210/7202/6006/KLIM_m63_lisa3.pdf); [RT audit annex citing reg. 63](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/aktilisa/1201/0202/6002/REM_12102026_m60_Lisa2.pdf)
- **EE:** the national calculation follows EN ISO 52000-1 (the ministry published an ISO 52000-1 Annex A national template) — [kliimaministeerium.ee ISO 52000-1 Annex A](https://kliimaministeerium.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2023-06/ISO_52000_1%3B2017_Annex%20A.pdf)
- **LV:** Cabinet Regulation No. 222 (8 Apr 2021) "Methods for calculating the energy performance of buildings and regulations for energy certification of buildings" — [REHVA Journal 03/2026, EPBD implementation in Latvia](https://www.rehva.eu/rehva-journal/chapter/epbd-implementation-in-latvia)
- **LT:** Building Technical Regulation STR 2.01.02:2016 "Design and Certification of Energy Performance of Buildings" (Order D1-754, 11 Nov 2016), under the Law on Construction and the Law on Energy. The current methodology has been in force since Feb 2017 — [CA-EPBD Lithuania 2018](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598450195807/CA-EPBD-IV-Lithuania-2018.pdf). A consolidated version in force from 1 Jan 2026 exists on e-seimas — [e-seimas](https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/15767120a80711e68987e8320e9a5185/asr)

### Inferences
- DK's SBi 213 and FI's guideline are monthly quasi-steady-state methods close to EN ISO 13790/52016 monthly. They are the best fits for a shared monthly engine.
- SE's switch to Etal on 1 Oct 2026 means any SE implementation built on BBR/EP_pet is already "old rules". It is valid only for projects using the transition until 30 Sep 2027.

### Gaps
- Whether the EE regulation 58 method is hourly (dynamic simulation) or allows monthly was not confirmed.
- The LV Reg. 222 calculation basis (monthly ISO 52016?) was not confirmed.

## 2. Label scale and exact class thresholds

### Takeaway
DK, FI and EE publish absolute, area-dependent formulas or tables that can be implemented directly. SE classes are percentages of the new-build requirement, and SE added A0 (zero-emission building) from 25 May 2026. LV (A+..G, size bands) and LT (A++..G via C1/C2 ratios plus envelope and airtightness criteria) could not be fully extracted.

### Cited Findings
**Denmark** (dwellings, kWh/m²·yr primary energy, A = heated gross floor area). Source: [ENS methodology note, Bilag 3](https://ens.dk/media/7592/download)
- A2020: 27.0 (fixed). A2015: < 30.0 + 1000/A. A2010: < 52.5 + 1650/A. B: < 70.0 + 2200/A. C: < 110 + 3200/A. D: < 150 + 4200/A. E: < 190 + 5200/A. F: < 240 + 6500/A. G: > 240 + 6500/A.
- ENS: A2015 is the energy frame requirement for new buildings from 2015 to now, and A2020 is a voluntary, more ambitious class.
- Non-residential scale: A2020 33, A2015 < 41 + 1000/A, A2010 < 71.3 + 1650/A, and so on. This is not needed for SFH.
- BR18 §259 legal text: for dwellings, total delivered energy demand for heating, ventilation, cooling and DHW per m² heated floor area "højst være 30,0 kWh/m² pr. år tillagt 1.000 kWh pr. år divideret med det opvarmede etageareal" — [bygningsreglementet.dk §259](https://www.bygningsreglementet.dk/tekniske-bestemmelser/11/krav/259/)
- The guidance example confirms this: a 150 m² building gives a frame of 36.7 kWh/m²·yr — [BR18 guidance](https://www.bygningsreglementet.dk/media/mxzlfopn/energiforbrug.pdf)
- Historic classes: Bolius gives 27 (raised from 20 in 2018) for Bygningsklasse 2020 — [Bolius](https://www.bolius.dk/energiklasser-for-nye-enfamiliehuse-18383)
- The "27 + 1100/A" formula in the brief does not exist. 35 + 1100/A was the old BR06/BR10 "lavenergiklasse 1" — [Bolius](https://www.bolius.dk/energiklasser-for-nye-enfamiliehuse-18383)

**Sweden** (BFS 2007:4 as amended by BFS 2026:2, in force 25 May 2026). Source: [BFS 2026:2](https://rinfo.boverket.se/BFS2007-4/pdf/BFS2026-2.pdf)
- A ≤ 50 % of the new-build requirement. B > 50–≤ 75 %. C > 75–≤ 100 %. D > 100–≤ 135 %. E > 135–≤ 180 %. F > 180–≤ 235 %. G > 235 %.
- **A0** = zero-emission building (nollutsläppsbyggnad). It must meet all of these: the primary-energy number corresponds to class A; there is no on-site fossil combustion; the installations can respond to external signals.
- Boverket: the 1 Oct 2026 amendments to the declaration rules "innebär bland annat att skalan på byggnadens energiklassning ändras". This means the scale changed again from 1 Oct 2026 — [Boverket news](https://www.boverket.se/sv/om-boverket/nyheter-aktuellt/nyheter/energihushallning-och-energideklaration/)
- Illustrative old-rule numbers for småhus > 130 m²: class C = 69–90 kWh/m², which implies a requirement of 90 — [byggahus.se](https://byggahus.se/energiklass-a-g-ta-reda-pa-husets-energiklass-och-om-uppgradering-ar-lonsam) (secondary source)

**Finland** (Annex 2 of 1048/2017, small residential buildings, use class 1 a–c: detached, two-dwelling, linked houses; E-luku in kWh_E/(m²·a), rounded up to an integer). Source: [Finlex 1048/2017](https://finlex.fi/api/media/statute/53865/mainPdf/main.pdf)
- For 50 ≤ A_netto ≤ 150 m²:
  - A ≤ 110 − 0.2·A
  - B ≤ 215 − 0.6·A
  - C ≤ 252 − 0.6·A
  - D ≤ 332 − 0.6·A
  - E ≤ 462 − 0.6·A
  - F ≤ 532 − 0.6·A
  - G above that
  - The B and C upper bounds are read from the next class's lower bound, because the PDF text extraction dropped them.
- For 150 < A_netto ≤ 600 m²:
  - A ≤ 83 − 0.02·A
  - B ≤ 131 − 0.04·A
  - C ≤ 173 − 0.07·A
  - D ≤ 253 − 0.07·A
  - E ≤ 383 − 0.07·A
  - F ≤ 453 − 0.07·A
  - G above that
- For A_netto > 600 m²: A ≤ 70, B 71–106, C 107–130, D 131–210, E 211–340, F 341–410, G > 410. The A value of 70 is inferred from the "B 71" lower bound.
- Row houses (1d) use fixed bands A ≤ 80, B 81–110, C 111–150, D 151–210, E 211–340, F 341–410, G ≥ 411.

**Estonia** (annex 3 of reg. 36, in the wording of regulation 63 of 09.07.2026; ETA or KEK in kWh/(m²·a)). Source: [RT annex 3](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/aktilisa/1210/7202/6006/KLIM_m63_lisa3.pdf)

| Category | A | B | C | D | E | F | G |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small residential (väikeelamu) < 120 m² | ≤ 135 | 136–160 | 161–185 | 186–235 | 236–285 | 286–350 | > 350 |
| Small residential 120–220 m² and row houses | ≤ 120 | 121–140 | 141–160 | 161–210 | 211–260 | 261–330 | > 330 |
| Small residential > 220 m² | ≤ 100 | 101–120 | 121–140 | 141–200 | 201–250 | 251–320 | > 320 |

- An older version of the same annex had A ≤ 145 for < 120 m², so the thresholds were tightened in 2026 — [search summary of RT annex versions](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/aktilisa/1070/7202/0013/Lisa_3.pdf) (not opened)

**Latvia**
- Two indicators are rated: space-heating demand, A+ to F, and non-renewable primary energy, A to G. Thresholds depend on heated area in three bands: 50–120 m², 120–250 m² and > 250 m². A+ is voluntary — [REHVA Journal 03/2026](https://www.rehva.eu/rehva-journal/chapter/epbd-implementation-in-latvia)
- Pre-2021 (Reg. 383, superseded) residential heating classes: A ≤ 40, B 40–60, C 60–80, D 80–100, E 100–150, F > 150 kWh/m²·a — [CA-EPBD Latvia 2020](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598706072870/Implementation-of-the-EPBD-in-the-Republic-of-Latvia--Status-in-2020.pdf) (search summary)

**Lithuania**
- Nine classes: A++, A+, A, B, C, D, E, F, G. A++ = nZEB — [CA-EPBD LT 2018](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598450195807/CA-EPBD-IV-Lithuania-2018.pdf)
- The class is determined from several parameters together:
  - specific heat loss of the envelope (U-values: walls ≤ 0.10·κ for A++, roof 0.080·κ, windows 0.70·κ)
  - airtightness test (A and above)
  - heat recovery efficiency
  - heating demand in kWh/m²
  - indicators C1 (non-renewable primary energy for heating, ventilation, cooling and lighting, relative to a reference) and C2 (non-renewable PE for DHW)
  - RES share (A++)
  - Source: [CA-EPBD LT 2018](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598450195807/CA-EPBD-IV-Lithuania-2018.pdf)
- An amendment text gives A++ as C1 < 0.3 and C2 ≤ 0.70 — [e-seimas amendment](https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/rs/legalact/TAD/b25875b124ac11edb36fa1cf41a91fd9/) (search snippet; fragmentary)

### Inferences
- DK, FI and EE map directly from a single kWh/m² number. FI and DK need A as an input, because the class boundary depends on floor area.
- LT is not a single-number scale. C1 and C2 are ratios to a national reference, and the class also needs airtightness, U-value and RES checks. A simple engine can only approximate it.

### Gaps
- LV Reg. 222 class thresholds (Figure 1 in REHVA is an image; the em.gov.lv consolidated .doc could not be parsed).
- LT full C1/C2 class table (A+ … G).
- The SE scale after 1 Oct 2026 (Etal-based) and the new småhus requirement value in BFS 2026:9.

## 3. Indicator and reference floor area

### Takeaway
All six are primary-energy-type indicators (FI calls it "weighted purchased energy", SE now Etal). Areas differ: DK heated gross floor area; SE Atemp; FI A_netto (net heated area); EE heated area (toatemperatuuriga pind); LV heated area; LT heated (useful) area.

### Cited Findings
- DK: delivered energy for heating, ventilation, cooling and DHW, weighted by energy factors, per m² "opvarmet etageareal" (heated gross floor area incl. heated basements in the floor area) — [BR18 §259](https://www.bygningsreglementet.dk/tekniske-bestemmelser/11/krav/259/); [BR18 guidance](https://www.bygningsreglementet.dk/media/mxzlfopn/energiforbrug.pdf)
- SE: EP_pet is computed in four steps. Energy for heating, DHW, comfort cooling and property electricity is multiplied by weighting factors. The heating energy is divided by Fgeo. The result is divided by Atemp. Household electricity is excluded — [Boverket](https://www.boverket.se/sv/byggande/bygg-och-renovera-energieffektivt/energihushallningskrav/primarenergital-och-byggnadens-energiprestanda/)
- FI: E-luku is weighted calculated purchased energy for standardised use, divided by A_netto (sum of heated storey areas measured to the inner surfaces of external walls). On-site renewables reduce purchased energy, and export is not credited — [Finlex 1048/2017](https://finlex.fi/api/media/statute/53865/mainPdf/main.pdf)
- EE: classes are by heated-area band (toatemperatuuriga pind), using ETA (calculated) or KEK (measured weighted specific use) — [RT annex 3](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/aktilisa/1210/7202/6006/KLIM_m63_lisa3.pdf)
- LV: non-renewable PE covers DHW, ventilation (fans, frost protection) and cooling (plus lighting in non-residential), in addition to heating — [REHVA 03/2026](https://www.rehva.eu/rehva-journal/chapter/epbd-implementation-in-latvia)
- LT: C1 covers non-renewable PE for heating, ventilation, cooling and lighting. C2 covers non-renewable PE for DHW — [CA-EPBD LT 2018](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598450195807/CA-EPBD-IV-Lithuania-2018.pdf)

### Gaps
- The exact SE Atemp definition text was not retrieved. It is commonly defined as the area heated to more than 10 °C inside the envelope, but this was not verified here.

## 4. Reference climate data

### Takeaway
DK uses one national DRY. FI computes E-luku for every site with climate zone I (Helsinki-Vantaa) weather. SE uses actual location data but divides heating by Fgeo, so results are location-neutral. EE uses a single national test reference year (not verified). Baltic details beyond EE are not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- DK: degree-hours and solar gain from the Danish Design Reference Year (DRY) — [BR18 guidance](https://www.bygningsreglementet.dk/media/mxzlfopn/energiforbrug.pdf)
- FI: E-luku is calculated with the weather data of climate zone I (Helsinki-Vantaa) — [Finlex 1048/2017](https://finlex.fi/api/media/statute/53865/mainPdf/main.pdf)
- SE: heating energy is divided by a geographic adjustment factor so that identical buildings get the same primärenergital anywhere in the country — [Boverket](https://www.boverket.se/sv/byggande/bygg-och-renovera-energieffektivt/energihushallningskrav/primarenergital-och-byggnadens-energiprestanda/)
- The DK/EE/FI comparison notes that differences in climate, methods and PE factors prevent direct comparison of nZEB levels. The study applied climate correction via degree-days — [Simson et al. 2022, RoomVent](https://vbn.aau.dk/ws/files/485547054/e3sconf_roomvent2022_01017.pdf)

### Inferences
- Because DK and FI fix a single national climate, and SE neutralises climate via Fgeo, a shared engine needs only one climate file per country (for SE, one reference climate plus division by the municipality's Fgeo).

### Gaps
- Fgeo values per municipality, the EE/LV/LT reference years, and whether the 1 Oct 2026 SE method keeps Fgeo were not retrieved.

## 5. Primary energy / weighting factors

### Cited Findings
- **DK:** electricity 1.9, district heating 0.85 — [Simson et al. 2022 Table 1](https://vbn.aau.dk/ws/files/485547054/e3sconf_roomvent2022_01017.pdf). Electricity 1.9 is also stated in the BR18 guidance — [BR18 guidance](https://www.bygningsreglementet.dk/media/mxzlfopn/energiforbrug.pdf) and the ENS note — [ENS](https://ens.dk/media/7592/download). Gas: not retrieved (other factors are 1.0 per BR18 §§251–253, unverified).
- **SE (old BBR):** electricity 1.8, district heating 0.7. Fossil fuels also have high factors. New factors were introduced on 1 Sep 2020 — [Boverket](https://www.boverket.se/sv/byggande/bygg-och-renovera-energieffektivt/energihushallningskrav/primarenergital-och-byggnadens-energiprestanda/). Factors under the 1 Oct 2026 Etal method: not found.
- **FI** (VNa 788/2017): electricity 1.2, district heat 0.5, district cooling 0.28, fossil fuels 1.0, renewable fuels used in the building 0.5 — [Finlex 1048/2017](https://finlex.fi/api/media/statute/53865/mainPdf/main.pdf)
- **EE:** electricity 2.0, district heating 0.65 — [Simson et al. 2022 Table 1](https://vbn.aau.dk/ws/files/485547054/e3sconf_roomvent2022_01017.pdf). For comparison, the EC recommendation is 2.3 for electricity and 1.3 for district heating (same source).
- LV, LT: not retrieved. The LT regulation sets non-renewable and renewable PEFs. The average non-renewable PEF for PV was studied at about 1.038 — [VilniusTech paper](https://vilniustech.lt/files/5098/254/12/11_0/-892.pdf) (search summary).

- The EE 2022 factors may have been revised in the 2024–2026 amendments of regulation 58 (the [TTJA change summary, Dec 2024](https://ttja.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2024-12/Muudatused%20energiat%C3%B5hususe%20m%C3%A4%C3%A4rustes%20ja%20laadimistaritu%20ning%20tehnos%C3%BCsteemi%20energiat%C3%B5hususe%20hinnang_0.pdf) exists but was not read).

## 6. Absolute vs relative thresholds

### Cited Findings
- DK: absolute formulas (constant + k/A) — [ENS](https://ens.dk/media/7592/download)
- SE: relative, as a percentage of the EP_pet requirement for a new building of the same type and size — [BFS 2026:2](https://rinfo.boverket.se/BFS2007-4/pdf/BFS2026-2.pdf)
- FI: absolute, with linear area terms — [Finlex](https://finlex.fi/api/media/statute/53865/mainPdf/main.pdf)
- EE: absolute tables by area band — [RT annex 3](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/aktilisa/1210/7202/6006/KLIM_m63_lisa3.pdf)
- LV: absolute values by area band (per REHVA description) — [REHVA](https://www.rehva.eu/rehva-journal/chapter/epbd-implementation-in-latvia)
- LT: relative (C1/C2 ratios to a reference) plus prescriptive criteria — [CA-EPBD LT 2018](https://cdn1.gopublic.dk/epbd/Media/638373598450195807/CA-EPBD-IV-Lithuania-2018.pdf)

### Inferences
- SE is easy to map once the requirement value for småhus is known. Under the old BBR this was 90, 95 or 100 kWh/m² depending on Atemp band (unverified this session).

The scale was changed again on 1 Oct 2026, and older declarations are "not fully comparable" — [Boverket](https://www.boverket.se/sv/om-boverket/nyheter-aktuellt/nyheter/energihushallning-och-energideklaration/)

## 9. Recommendation

### Takeaway
- **DK — KEEP.** Fully published, absolute area formulas, monthly SBi 213 method with a single DRY. A 150 m² house in class A2015 is ≤ 36.7 kWh/m². Check whether the 2025/26 BR changes for the EPBD altered the A2020/A2015 labels.
- **FI — KEEP.** Absolute E-luku formulas by A_netto, a single zone-I climate and simple published factors (1.2/0.5/1.0). Watch the 2026 decree amendment.
- **EE — KEEP.** Clean absolute tables updated July 2026 with three SFH size bands. PE factors (2.0/0.65) need confirming against the current reg. 58.
- **SE — UNCERTAIN.** The relative % scale plus A0 is easy to map, but the Etal method and a new scale took effect on 1 Oct 2026, and their numbers are not yet documented. The old EP_pet/Fgeo version is valid only through the transition (30 Sep 2027).
- **LV — UNCERTAIN.** The structure is known (two indicators, three area bands, A+..G), but the threshold values were not obtained from a parseable source.
- **LT — DROP** (or approximate only). Classes need C1/C2 ratios to a national reference plus prescriptive U-value, airtightness, heat recovery and RES criteria. A single kWh/m² from a generic engine cannot reproduce them.

### Inferences
- Of the "absolute + monthly-friendly" countries, DK, FI and EE are the strongest candidates. SE can be added as "old-rules" mode with % mapping if a requirement value is supplied.
